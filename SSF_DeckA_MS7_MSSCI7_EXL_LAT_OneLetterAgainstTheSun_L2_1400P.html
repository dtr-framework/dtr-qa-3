THE SCENE

You keep the notebook for a potato study high in the Andes. You are alone in the tent, and one page is still empty. That page is about one change in a potato gene. You need four notes to finish it. Four people each can give you one.

THE HISTORY

People in the Andes have grown potatoes for thousands of years. Quechua families near Cusco still keep more than a thousand kinds. They save potatoes from the plants that do well. Their potato park sits high where the sun is strong.

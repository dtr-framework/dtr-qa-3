THE SCENE
Your class runs the weather desk at the port for one day. You are alone in the radio room. The boats leave at seven, and the wind board must be done first. You need four numbers, and four people each have one.
THE HISTORY
For a long time, sailors in Oman watched the wind. For half the year it blew one way. Then it turned and blew back. They called it mawsim, the Arabic word for season.

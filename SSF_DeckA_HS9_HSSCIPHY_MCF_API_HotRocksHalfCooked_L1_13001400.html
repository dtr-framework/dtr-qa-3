THE SCENE

You solve science mysteries for your school. Yesterday your science club cooked food in an imu. When they opened it, the food was only half cooked. Some heat is missing. Four people each have one number you need.

THE HISTORY

This mystery starts with the imu, a Hawaiian ground oven. Native Hawaiians have cooked in it for hundreds of years. Hot rocks and food go into a hole. Dirt covers the hole and keeps the heat inside.

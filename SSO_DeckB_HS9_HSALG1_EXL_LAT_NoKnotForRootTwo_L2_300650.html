Quipu Team Must Choose
The Old Road or the Shortcut

Twelve days on the road. One path for the whole trip.

A school team will walk an old Inca road for twelve days. The village asked them to record the trip on a quipu. A quipu is a set of cords with knots for whole numbers. The old road is exactly 20 kilometers a day. The shortcut is 10 times the square root of 2 kilometers. That is about 14 kilometers, but no knot can show it exactly.

THE QUESTION BEFORE US

Which is better for the team, the old road or the shortcut?
